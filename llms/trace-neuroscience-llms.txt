# Trace Neuroscience

> Trace Neuroscience, Inc. is a biotechnology company developing genetic medicines for neurodegenerative diseases. Its lead investigational medicine, TRCN-1023, is designed to restore the function of the UNC13A protein, which is disrupted in most people living with amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS). The company is running the FUNCtion ALS trial (NCT07674667), a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled Phase 1/2 study of TRCN-1023 delivered by intrathecal injection.
